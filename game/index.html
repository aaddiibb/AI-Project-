CONTROLS

Ultimate bar fills when you attack and when you get hit or block: the harder you are hit, the more you gain. Guard gauge (thin bar below): blocked hits fill it, it drains when you stop blocking; full = GUARD BREAK (long stun).
SAMURAI (P1): A D walk · Shift dash (+back = grounded back-dash, also double-tap back)
J iai cut (mid) · K stepping thrust (gap closer) · L overhead cleave (chip, knockdown) · U Crimson Tempest (full meter) · Space block (standing only)
Repel: hold Space + tap J — ANYTIME (even knocked down or in a combo): a blade shockwave shoves her away. 25 meter, short cooldown.
Parry: tap Space just as a kunai arrives to deflect it (no damage), then K to thrust in. Chains: iai cut → thrust → cleave.
Crimson Tempest: three waves (high, low, tall). A clean hit starts the punishment cinematic. Ultimates that meet cancel each other.
He cannot jump, crouch, run or throw: those keys do nothing.
A D walk / double-tap forward run / double-tap back hop back
W jump (tap = light, hold = high) · S crouch
J light sword · K kick · L heavy slash · I kunai throw · U ultimate (full meter)
Space block (hold) · Shift dash (+back = hop)
Kawarimi: hold Space + tap J — if a sword hits the seal she leaves a log and strikes from behind. Costs 25 meter; whiff = open; short cooldown; melee only. Escape: the same input while being hit, blocking, knocked down or getting up = instant substitution that puts distance between you (25 meter).
S+J crouch light sword · S+K sweep · in the air: J dive stab, K flying kick
Normals chain upward after they connect: light sword → kick → slash → kunai. Meter fills when your attacks connect.
Arrow keys also work. Esc menu · H AI thinking overlay · G hit boxes · F1 show/hide this.
