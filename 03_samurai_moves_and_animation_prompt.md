### Sword and scabbard states (get this right)
Use `pose.attach` as in the Shinobi's sword poses: the sword is at rest in the saya (`attach.sword = rig.attachments.sword.rest`) in every pose **except** `iai_slash`, `iai_recover`, `heavy_*`, `ult_windup`, `ult_release`, `ult_follow`, `ult_flourish` (drawn, attached to the hand grip; `attachRot` as needed). For the heavy attack, the second hand must sit on the hilt. If the runtime attaches one grip, pose the far hand onto the hilt by rotation and say so in the README.

### Craft rules
- **Heavy, deliberate motion.** Compared with the Shinobi: bigger anticipation, slower returns, weight dropped into the lunge, cloth lagging behind.
- Keep **feet planted on the same ground line** in every grounded pose (ground lock does this; verify visually).
- Each attack must read at **440 px** tall: strong silhouette on windup and strike, clear line of action.
- Faces: `face_neutral` default; `face_intense` on `iai_*`, `heavy_*`, `ult_*`; `face_hurt` on `hit` and KO poses.
- `springHang` low for lying poses so cloth and hair do not float.

## 4. The ultimate (design proposal; keep it simple)

Game rules (do not change): needs a full meter, **unblocked about 50% of max HP (500)**, **blocked about 15-20% (150-200)**, startup is **not invulnerable**, the world freezes briefly at the start (`freeze`, played with `flash` poses).

**Samurai ultimate: "Crimson Crescent".** After the freeze he draws and releases one huge crimson crescent of energy that travels along the ground in front of him.

| Parameter | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Frames | `ult_charge` 6, `ult_windup` 12, `ult_release` 8, `ult_follow` 14, `stance` 8; the projectile spawns at frame 18 (`spawnAt`) |
| Projectile | one wide crescent, speed about 34, life 44 (range about 1500), hit box about 160 wide, y 0..420 (low: the Shinobi can jump over it with good timing; blocking works; crouching does **not** save him) |
| Damage | 500 unblocked, `chip` 175 blocked, `noScale: true`, knockdown |
| Visual | large red/white crescent drawn by the engine from the sword's `arc` path at release plus a projectile sprite; **you** provide the poses and the `sword_tip` / `sword_guard` anchors; the engine draws the effect |

If you think a different delivery fits better (a long-range dash-through cut, a screen-wide horizontal slash), propose it **with the same damage budget** and explain the counterplay (block / jump / distance). Do not add invulnerability.
