Offline audio render of a replay

Plays an arena replay through the game's real audio engine, rendered faster than real time into an OfflineAudioContext: the same cues, mix, ducking, reverb and music as in the game. Use it to listen to a whole match, check the loudness targets and confirm that a replay always sounds the same.
?replay=../replays/demo_minimax_vs_mcts.json chooses the log; serve the project with npm run serve and open http://localhost:8767/game/tools/audio_render.html.
